Transport Terminal Management & ANPR System

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Role Responsibilities Guide

Core Principle: This system is NOT a parking management tool. Vehicles operate under Weekly Roster Assignments. ANPR cameras detect arriving and departing vehicles to automate queue entry, manage FIFO yard loading, calculate turnaround duration, collect dispatch fees, and prevent unauthorized terminal operations.

1. End-to-End Operational Lifecycle (Chronological Order)

1
Master Data & Hardware Setup (System Admin)
Terminals, routes, vehicle fleets, user RBAC, dispatch fee tariffs, and ANPR cameras are configured in the system.
2
Weekly Route Scheduling (Municipal Planner)
Rosters are auto-generated round-robin, conflicts are resolved, dispatchers are assigned, and the schedule is published and locked.
3
Terminal Gate Entry & Verification (ANPR / Dispatcher)
Entry ANPR camera scans arriving plate. If authorized on the active weekly roster, vehicle enters FIFO queue as WAITING. If wrong terminal, marked as VIOLATION.
4
Dispatch In & Yard Boarding (Dispatcher)
Dispatcher confirms vehicle arrival into the loading bay (LOADING). Passengers board the vehicle.
5
Exit Gate Scan, Fee Collection & Dispatch Out (ANPR / Dispatcher)
Exit ANPR detects vehicle (DETECTED_AT_EXIT). Dispatcher collects the dispatch fee (Telebirr/CBE Birr/Cash), inputs receipt reference, confirms departure, barrier opens, and status changes to ON_ROUTE.
6
Auditing & Analytics (System Admin / Planner)
Every transaction is stamped with an immutable audit reference (AUD-XXXX). Performance and financial reports are generated.

2. Detailed Role Responsibilities (In Order of Action)

ROLE 1 System Administrator (Super Admin)

Focus: System governance, infrastructure configuration, security, user access, and tariff rules.

1
User & Access Management (/users, /roles)
Create user accounts (Planners, Dispatchers, Supervisors), assign users to their designated physical terminals, and configure permissions.
2
Terminal & Route Setup (/terminals, /routes)
Register physical terminals (GPS coordinates, bay capacity, operational status) and create inter-terminal routes (distance, estimated transit time).
3
Fleet & Group Setup (/vehicle-groups, /vehicles)
Group vehicles into operational clusters (Group A, Group B) and register fleet vehicles with license plates, models, and seating capacity.
4
ANPR Hardware & Device Onboarding (/cameras, /devices)
Configure Entry and Exit gate ANPR cameras (RTSP URLs, IP addresses, OCR confidence thresholds) and approve handheld mobile devices.
5
Fee Tariffs & Policies (/dispatch-fees, /settings)
Define departure dispatch fee rules by vehicle type (e.g., Minibus = 25 ETB, Coaster = 50 ETB, Bus = 75 ETB) and configure system parameters.
6
Overrides & Audit Logs (/overrides, /audit-logs)
Review emergency override requests using Supervisor PIN and inspect immutable audit trails.

ROLE 2 Municipal Planner

Focus: Strategic scheduling, weekly roster lifecycle, fleet distribution, and capacity planning.

1
Demand & Performance Analysis (/dashboard, /reports)
Inspect historical route passenger demand, peak gate hours, and vehicle group utilization.
2
Auto-Generate / Create Weekly Roster (/rosters)
Select operating week (e.g. Week 38) and click 'Auto-Generate Schedule' to pair vehicle groups with active routes round-robin with automatic conflict prevention.
3
Review & Dispatcher Validation (/rosters)
Review draft assignments and verify assigned dispatchers belong to origin or destination terminals. Status: [DRAFT] → [REVIEWED].
4
Publish Weekly Roster (/rosters)
Publish schedule ([PUBLISHED]). Gate ANPR cameras immediately begin recognizing vehicles belonging to scheduled groups.
5
Lock Authoritative Roster (/rosters)
Freeze roster into official record ([LOCKED]). Edits are blocked; unassigned vehicle arrivals trigger automated gate violations.
6
Archive Completed Rosters (/rosters)
Archive completed rosters ([ARCHIVED]) at the end of the operating week.

ROLE 3 Terminal Dispatcher (Live Operations)

Focus: Live gate execution, check-in, queue sequencing, passenger boarding, fee collection, and gate barrier control.

1
Monitor Live Console & Recognition Feed (/queue, /recognition/dashboard)
Open the real-time FIFO queue console for their assigned terminal. Monitor optical recognition scans and audio alerts.
2
Vehicle Arrival Verification (/queue → 'Incoming / Pending In')
ANPR scans plate. If assigned on weekly roster, enters FIFO queue as WAITING. If camera is offline, dispatcher uses 'Manual Check-In'.
3
Dispatch In → Loading Yard (/queue → 'Loading Yard')
When vehicle enters the loading bay, dispatcher clicks 'Dispatch In'. State changes to LOADING while passengers board.
4
Exit Gate ANPR Detection (/queue → 'Exit Gate / Pending Out')
Loaded vehicle drives to exit gate. Exit ANPR camera scans plate and automatically flags vehicle as DETECTED_AT_EXIT.
5
Fee Collection & Dispatch Out (/queue)
Dispatcher verifies fee (e.g. 25 ETB), selects payment method (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Cash, Electronic), inputs Transaction ID, and confirms. Gate opens and vehicle state becomes ON_ROUTE.
6
Resolve Gate Violations (/violations or /queue → 'Violations Review')
Evaluate unassigned or wrong-terminal vehicles: (A) Admit / Override with supervisor remark, or (B) Turn Away / Reject.
